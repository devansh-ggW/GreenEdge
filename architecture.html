01 — Architecture

Context-driven spaces, designed around real life.

Architecture at GreenEdge starts with the site and the people who will use it — its climate, orientation and constraints, and the everyday rhythms of the lives that will fill it.

How we think about a building

A building is shaped as much by what surrounds it as by what's asked of it. Orientation, prevailing light, existing structures and the movement of people around a site all inform the earliest sketches, well before questions of style or finish come up.

From there, the plan is developed around how the space will actually be used day to day — where people gather, where they pass through, and where they need quiet. Structure, material and detail are worked out so the building holds together as one coherent idea, not a collection of separate decisions.

What shapes the design
  • Site orientation, climate and existing conditions
  • How the building will be used, day to day
  • Structure, material and construction logic
  • Its relationship to interiors and landscape
  • Light, ventilation and long-term maintenance
